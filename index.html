Quality assurance

QA tester at XP Games.

I break things on purpose. My testing covers multiplayer host and client, save and reload persistence, and fresh versus existing world updates, with an eye on Bedrock regressions, multiplayer desyncs and Marketplace certification. Every report includes isolated repro steps, expected versus actual results, a severity tag, and video or screenshot evidence.

About

I'm Manith Liyanage, known as Setonix. I turn loose ideas into clear plans, and clear plans into released products.

As a project lead I document, balance and create game concepts, and oversee builders, modellers and animators so tasks land on time. Having worked every step myself, I know how much time, effort and manpower a project really needs.

I focus on letting the team be as creative as they can, while making sure the work gets done in a timely, consistent way.

Outside games, I was a director at a recruitment firm in Colombo, handling finances, clients, marketing and operations.
