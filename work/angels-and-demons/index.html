Overview

A story map about two factions, played as someone caught between them.

Angels & Demons is a story-driven Minecraft adventure map for 1 to 12 players. The Aether realm has fallen to demon corruption. You play a Broken Angel, thrown from the sky and fused with both light and shadow. You win Aether weapons back from ruined sanctuaries and take Demon weapons from the lords you kill.

I wrote the game design: story, levels, weapons, enemies, NPCs, UI and VFX, plus a technical overview for the developers to build from. Then I worked with the modeller, builder, animator and VFX artist in their channels until every asset matched the doc.

03

Two factions

Every asset belongs to a side.

I gave each faction its own look, so players can tell at a glance whether something is light or dark.

Aether

Light magic, floating temples and sacred relics.

  • Glowing weapons with particle trails
  • Shields, healing fields, stun rays
  • Gold hotbar and yellow hearts in the HUD

Demon

Shadow, fire and corruption.

  • Jagged weapons with black flames
  • Burn zones, teleport slashes, lifesteal
  • Cracked red-black hotbar with fang hunger icons
04

Levels

Six chapters, each with its own job.

Each level has a different objective, like defending a beacon, protecting crystal cores or fighting a boss. That way the fights don't all feel the same. Pick a chapter.

From doc to build

05

Weapons

Eight weapons, each with one signature ability.

For each weapon I wrote its appearance, damage, particles, and the player animation as well as the weapon animation. The spear briefs say the player twirls it, throws with a full-body twist and catches it on the way back.

06

Bestiary

Enemies with clear stats and clear roles.

Every creature has health, speed, two attacks and a full animation set: idle, move, attack, skill, damage and death. The bars show health against the Mound King's 300.

Each NPC got an idle animation with small personal details, plus its own particle notes. Glinz glitters with gold, Seraphiel shimmers, and Umbrex trails black-red dust.

09

Thought process

  1. 01

    A hero from both sides

    Making the player half Aether and half Demon lets them use weapons from both factions. That adds replay value, and it ties the plot twist to who the player is.

  2. 02

    Choice through scarcity

    One weapon per level at the shop makes every purchase a real decision instead of a checklist.

  3. 03

    Scale without lag

    Spawn multipliers by player count, with hard mob caps, keep a 12-player game busy without breaking performance.

  4. 04

    Spec the player as well as the weapon

    Writing first-person and third-person player animations into each weapon brief saved a round of back-and-forth with the animator.

  5. 05

    Stay close to the team

    I answered artists in their own channels, so questions were settled quickly and the doc stayed the single source of truth.
